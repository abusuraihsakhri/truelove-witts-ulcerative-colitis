Assessment

Systemic toxicity markers

    Interpretation

    Severe: At least six stools/day with visible blood, plus any one of pulse >90/min, temperature >37.8°C, haemoglobin <10.5 g/dL, ESR >30 mm/hour or CRP >30 mg/L. Mild: Fewer than four stools/day, none/small blood, and no abnormality in the assessed mild-range markers. Moderate: Between the mild and severe patterns.

    Indeterminate: Six or more visibly bloody stools with negative measured toxicity markers but missing ESR or CRP. Missing values are never treated as negative tests.

    References: NICE NG130 and BSG guidelines (2025).
